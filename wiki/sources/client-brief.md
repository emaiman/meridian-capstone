# Client brief: Meridian Markets

**What it is:** a letter from Dana Okafor (VP of Operations, Meridian Markets) to the LMU MSBA workshop team, dated August 2026. Tier: **Allowed**. It contains no customer or employee data, so it may be read and summarized with AI tools.

## Key points

- **Company:** specialty grocery chain with 14 stores across Los Angeles, Orange, and Ventura counties, about $78M in annual revenue, about 620 employees. Competes on prepared foods, local sourcing, and a smaller footprint than national chains.
- **Growth:** from 6 to 14 stores in 5 years, mostly by taking over leases from chains that left neighborhoods Meridian saw as underserved.
- **The ask:** a dashboard of sales performance by store and by category so leadership can decide where to open the next location.
- **Pasadena:** described as "the obvious next step"; leadership wants data to back it up before committing.
- **Why now:** growth has been uneven (some stores took off immediately, others were slower). Decisions so far were made "on instinct and a spreadsheet."
- **Loyalty:** about 40,000 members; Dana believes the data has never really been used. She wants to understand the customer better.
- **Success:** "increase revenue, reduce operating costs, and improve the customer experience," through better visibility into which stores and categories perform.
- **Data available:** about three years of point-of-sale transactions; loyalty membership and purchase history; labor scheduling and hours; store attributes (square footage, opening date, lease terms).
- **POS migration:** Meridian moved to a new POS system last spring.
- **Access:** IT (Marcus) can pull an extract once the NDA is signed.
- **Terms of engagement:** customer records and employee data (loyalty, labor schedules, and any excerpts) must not go into any AI tool. Sales totals by store and week, and store attributes, are fine to use. Counsel is firm; not negotiable.
- **Timeline:** about eight weeks, plus something preliminary for the board in three weeks.
- **Contact:** email is best. Dana travels Tuesdays and Wednesdays and is slow to reply; her assistant can schedule but cannot answer analytics questions.
