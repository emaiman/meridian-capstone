# JLL: Grocery Report 2025

**What it is:** an annual grocery market report by JLL's retail research and advisory leads (James Cook and Naveen Jaggi), published 2025-02-18. Tier: **Allowed** (public report, no client data). JLL is a commercial real estate advisor, so the report is strongest on real estate and traffic and less relevant to store operations. Cautions:

- **Dated.** The figures are for 2024 and were about 19 months old when ingested. The [[icsc-new-grocery-formats]] article (April 2026) quotes the same author.
- **Extract, not verbatim.** The raw file is a fetched summary. Check figures against the original before using them outside the wiki.

## Key points

- **Space is scarce.** Grocery-anchored vacancy was 3.5% (Q4 2024), net new supply was under 100,000 sq ft for a second year, and rents grew 3.1%, the fastest of any retail type. The report expects "minimal new supply additions" and vacancy near record lows.
- **Traffic is healthy, and discount and specialty chains lead.** Grocery visits were 17.2 billion in 2024. Aldi visits were up 51.2% since 2019, Grocery Outlet 48.7%, and Trader Joe's 24.7%. Sprouts opened 35 stores and Trader Joe's 34 in 2024.
- **Competition is consolidating.** Regional grocers are scaling through acquisitions, including Jim Pattison Group's purchase of Save Mart (190+ West Coast stores). Smaller formats are used to enter markets.
- **Shoppers hunt for deals.** Multi-store shopping and private-label trading down are rising, and dining out now outspends grocery.
- **Regional note.** The report calls out West Coast activity (Save Mart, Amazon Fresh) and lists Vallarta Markets among delivery partners, so Southern California competitors are named.

## What this changes for the interview

- **Site availability may be the binding constraint.** With little supply and rising rents, Meridian's habit of taking over vacated leases ([[client-brief]]) may face more competition for sites. See [[open-questions]] #14 and [[assumptions]] A8.

- **Multi-store shopping** suggests customers may split trips, which affects how Meridian's loyalty data should be read. Any Meridian loyalty data stays Restricted under `data-handling-checklist.md`.
