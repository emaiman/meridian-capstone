# Open questions

Gaps and ambiguities in the brief, ranked by how much they change the work. These feed the interview guide.

| Rank | Question | Why it matters | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | What does "success" mean here: revenue, margin, payback time, or something else? The brief lists "increase revenue, reduce operating costs, improve customer experience." | The dashboard and the site recommendation depend on the target metric. | [[client-brief]] |
| 2 | Why is Pasadena "the obvious next step," and what alternatives were considered? | Determines whether the analysis is validating one site or comparing several. | [[client-brief]], [[pasadena]] |
| 3 | Can aggregates derived from loyalty or labor data be used with AI tools, and who confirms that in writing? | The brief bars "any excerpts"; aggregates are Restricted until Dana confirms. Affects how we can analyze loyalty data. | [[client-brief]] |

| 5 | What caused uneven performance across stores: location, size, timing, or category mix? | The main pattern the analysis must explain. | [[client-brief]] |

| 7 | Do POS transactions carry loyalty or cashier IDs? | Decides whether the POS extract is Allowed or Ask-first under the checklist. | [[client-brief]], [[marcus-it]] |
