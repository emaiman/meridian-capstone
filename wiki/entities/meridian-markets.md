# Meridian Markets

Specialty grocery chain. Client for this engagement.

## Profile

- 14 stores across Los Angeles, Orange, and Ventura counties ([[client-brief]]).
- About $78M in annual revenue and about 620 employees ([[client-brief]]).
- Competes on prepared foods, local sourcing, and a smaller footprint than national chains ([[client-brief]]).

## Growth

- Grew from 6 to 14 stores in 5 years, mostly by taking over leases from chains that pulled out of neighborhoods Meridian considered underserved ([[client-brief]]).
- Growth is uneven: some stores took off immediately, others were slower to find their footing ([[client-brief]]).
- Expansion decisions have been made "on instinct and a spreadsheet" ([[client-brief]]).

## Data and systems

- Data: about three years of POS transactions, loyalty (about 40,000 members), labor scheduling, and store attributes ([[client-brief]]).
- Moved to a new POS system last spring ([[client-brief]]). Effect on data continuity is unknown; see [[open-questions]].
