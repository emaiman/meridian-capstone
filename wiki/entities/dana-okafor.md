# Dana Okafor

VP of Operations, [[meridian-markets]]. Author of the brief and the interviewee for this wiki.

## What she has asked for

- A dashboard of sales performance by store and category to decide where to open the next location ([[client-brief]]).
- Data to back up the Pasadena site before leadership commits ([[client-brief]]; see [[pasadena]]).
- A better understanding of the customer using the loyalty data ([[client-brief]]).
- Something preliminary for the board in three weeks ([[client-brief]]).

## Working with her

- Email is best. She travels Tuesdays and Wednesdays and is slow to reply; silence is not a problem ([[client-brief]]).
- Her assistant can schedule time but cannot answer analytics questions ([[client-brief]]).
- She invites questions: "If anything here is unclear, ask" ([[client-brief]]).
- She has set a firm data rule: no customer or employee data in AI tools ([[client-brief]]).

## Implication

The interview may be the only real chance to get analytics answers directly, so questions must be prioritized. See [[open-questions]].
