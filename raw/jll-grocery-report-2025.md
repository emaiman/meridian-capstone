## Consumer and traffic

- Dining-out spending exceeded grocery spending by over $21 billion by end of 2024 (vs. $3.4 billion in January 2022).
- Grocery store foot traffic: 17.2 billion visits in 2024 (up 1.0% from 2023; up 10.9% from 2019). Average dwell time 23.4 minutes (down 0.3 minutes from 2023).
- Aldi: 900+ million visits in 2024 (up 51.2% from 2019). Grocery Outlet: 130 million (up 48.7%). Trader Joe's: 390 million (up 24.7%). Top 10 grocers captured 44% of industry visit share.
- Private-label sales over $270 billion in 2024 (up 3.4%); 20.7% market share, up 160 basis points from 2021.
- "Food at home" inflation 1.8% (December 2024). SNAP participants 41+ million in fiscal 2024. Grocery e-commerce $204 billion in 2024 (up 10.7%). Retail media ad spending $52 billion (up 20.4%).

## Real estate

- Grocery-anchored retail vacancy 3.5% (Q4 2024), vs. 3.6% (Q4 2023) and 4.5% at the Q2 2021 peak.
- Net absorption 300,000 sq ft in 2024 (down 37.9% from 2023). Net deliveries under 100,000 sq ft for the second year running.
- Annual rent growth 3.1% (Q4 2024), highest among retail types. Average price per sq ft a record $209 in 2024. Available retail space down 7.5 million sq ft since the Q1 2021 peak.
- Multi-tenant grocery-anchored transactions $7.0 billion in 2024 (up 1.4%); second-half volume up 34%. Private capital share fell from 74% (2023) to 68% (2024).

## Expansion (2024 openings) and deals

- Aldi: 105 new stores; announced 225+ for 2025 and 800 by 2028. Publix: 43. H-E-B: 9. Sprouts: 35. Trader Joe's: 34 (+450K sq ft). Amazon Fresh: 21. The Fresh Market: 8. Whole Foods: steady expansion.
- Kroger-Albertsons merger blocked (December 2024). Aldi acquired Southeastern Grocers' Winn-Dixie and Harveys stores (220). Jim Pattison Group completed its acquisition of Save Mart (190+ West Coast stores, June 2024).
- DoorDash partnerships in 2024 included Vallarta Markets and H Mart.

## Trends stated

- Site selection: an acute supply shortage limits grocer expansion; demand exceeds availability; rent growth is accelerating because of minimal new construction.
- Discount formats (Aldi, Grocery Outlet) are taking share; specialty grocers (Trader Joe's, Sprouts) are thriving with curated selection; smaller formats are a tool for market penetration; regional grocers are scaling through M&A.
- Consumers are deal-seeking and shop at multiple stores; trading down to private label is accelerating.
- Geographic emphasis: Southeast (Aldi, Publix), Texas (H-E-B), California and West Coast (Save Mart activity, Amazon Fresh).

## Outlook (as stated)

- "Minimal new supply additions" with persisting demand, keeping "vacancy and availabilities near record lows"; asking rents expected to keep rising.
- Retail media networks forecast at $100 billion a year by 2028; grocery e-commerce over $270 billion by 2028 (7.4% compound annual growth); discount grocer expansion expected to accelerate.
