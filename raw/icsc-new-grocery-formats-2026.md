## Market and investment

- The U.S. grocery sector is valued at $912.4 billion (IBISWorld estimate).
- Grocery-anchored center deals totaled nearly $11 billion in 2025, up 42% from 2024.

## Consumer shift (Consumer Edge, U.S. Grocery Outlook 2026)

- Trader Joe's grew 3% year over year, outperforming the sector by 6 percentage points, with double-digit growth among Gen Z shoppers.
- Traditional grocers such as Publix and Safeway declined across income groups.
- Discount grocer growth (Aldi, Food 4 Less) has plateaued since mid-2025.

## Company moves

- **Target:** repositioning its $24 billion grocery business toward specialty and differentiation; "investing meaningfully in the in-store food experience."
- **Walmart:** remodeling over 650 stores in 2026; about 20 openings in 2026-2027; $5.5 billion allocated for 675 remodels in fiscal 2026.
- **Costco:** targeting 30+ new stores a year; 28 net openings expected in fiscal 2026; nearly 640 U.S. warehouse stores.
- **Aldi:** piloting flexible modular store formats in Florida (began September 2025); opening 150 stores nationwide this year.
- **Gelson's:** launched ReCharge by Gelson's micromarkets (3,000 to 5,400 sq ft) paired with EV charging hubs in Southern California.

## Transactions (2025-2026)

- Brixton Capital: Las Palmas Village (Las Vegas, Vons-anchored) for $29.4 million; Quad at Whittier (Los Angeles, Vallarta-anchored) for $100 million.
- Medipower: 7-property, 558,000 sq ft portfolio for $115 million.
- Nuveen: 7-property, 956,865 sq ft portfolio (6 grocery-anchored) for $298 million.
- Blackstone Real Estate: three largely grocery-anchored portfolios (130 properties) for $6.7 billion.
- Regency Centers: developing a 71,400 sq ft Whole Foods-anchored center in Redlands, California.

## Trends

- Movement toward specialty grocers, fresh formats, and value concepts; smaller urban footprints; flexible modular designs replacing standardized layouts.
- JLL's Naveen Jaggi: "the days when any grocery store could anchor a center equally well are fading."
- Tillster 2026 Phygital Index: 29% of diners visit fast-food chains less often and 37% reduced fast-casual visits; 36% increased grocery store visits and 33% shopped c-stores more.
- JLL Income Property Trust's STARS tracks 40,000 grocery-anchored retail centers nationwide.
